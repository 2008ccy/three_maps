Sophisticated magazine design, Chinese Songti / Noto Serif CJK headline with a clean Chinese sans-serif for navigation and descriptions, no more than two font families. Fine rules, deliberate generous whitespace and strong type hierarchy. Crisp, practical, buildable UI, tasteful, custom-designed, not an AI SaaS landing page.
Layout: content spans from x100 to x1340. Top compact masthead logo “三张活地图” at left and small topic “模型构建”; navigation aligned right “领域  生态  认知  更新记录” with a modest search affordance “搜索地图”. A thin coral rule spans below. Right 40% a beautiful flat editorial knowledge-map plate, ivory paper, sparse charcoal nodes and thin lines, THREE labeled clusters “知识”“协作”“判断”, a few muted coral nodes. It should look like an actual intellectual atlas illustration, precisely drawn and uncluttered, without photorealistic objects.
Below the hero a small heading “从三张地图开始” with short coral underline. Three equal open columns across the width, vertically separated ONLY by fine light-gray rules, no enclosing cards or fills: “01 领域地图” / “学什么，怎样构建？”; “02 生态地图” / “谁在推动，如何协作？”; “03 认知地图” / “怎样判断，如何修正？”.
